## Brand & Style

This design system embodies spiritual tranquility, sacred reverence, and refined modern luxury. Designed specifically for a noble platform dedicated to Qur'anic memorization, revision (*wird*), and devotional tracking, the aesthetic fuses Islamic classical manuscript arts with modern minimalist ergonomics.

The target audience encompasses dedicated memorizers, students of knowledge, and individuals seeking mindful daily devotional habits. The visual environment must evoke stillness (*sakinah*), intellectual clarity, and profound visual comfort for prolonged recitation sessions.

### Design Movement: Modern Heritage & Tactile Editorial
- **Tactile Paper Warmth:** Surfaces mirror physical manuscript vellum and smooth artisanal paper rather than synthetic digital whites.
- **Micro-Geometric Arabesque Accents:** Restrained, precise geometric cues and delicate golden hairlines provide structural grounding without visual clutter.
- **Spacious Editorial Calm:** Generous margins, generous vertical rhythmic breathing room, and deliberate typographic hierarchy eliminate cognitive friction.
- **Dual-State Sacred Atmosphere:** Flawless transition between a sunlit parchment day mode and an eye-soothing, deep night slate for pre-dawn (*tahajjud*) and late-night recitations.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classical illuminated manuscripts: aged parchment, mineral emerald inks, brushed gold leaf, and deep nocturnal slate.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#0D3B2E` - Deep Emerald):** Serves as the bedrock brand color. Used for high-emphasis interactive states, primary action anchors, major header highlights, and completed progress nodes.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` - Sand Gold):** Represents attainment, ayah markers, active verse highlights, and micro-accents. Radiates warmth without metallic glare.
- **Tertiary (`#134E3F` - Luminous Pine):** Supports secondary surfaces, interactive hover states, badges, and subtle container strokes.
- **Neutral Backgrounds & Foundations:**
  - *Day Mode Base (`#FBF9F4` Ivory Vellum):* Replaces harsh digital white with natural tactile warmth.
  - *Day Mode Surface (`#F4EEE1` Paper Cream):* Elevated card containers, reading segments, and module groupings.
  - *Night Mode Canvas (`#0B131A` to `#162432` Deep Navy Slate):* Softens dark room contrast, eradicating eye strain during dawn and night worship.
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - *Day Mode On-Surface (`#1A2421`):* Deep soot black with a hint of forest green for natural optical contrast.
  - *Night Mode On-Surface (`#EAE5D9`):* Muted warm cream for glare-free scripture scanning.

## Typography

The typography architecture balances classical calligraphic poise with optimal digital clarity.

### Family Roles
- **Scripture & Editorial (`Newsreader` / Arabic Naskh Equivalent):** Provides solemnity and rhythmic cadence for Qur'anic text, Surah introductions, and meditative milestone titles. The line-height is intentionally expanded (`line-height: 2.0+`) to honor diacritical marks (*tashkeel*) and pause symbols (*waqf*).
- **Interface & Operational Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans` / Arabic Modern Sans Equivalent):** Delivers clean, unhindered readability for tracking logs, stats, settings, navigation, and time-sensitive reminders.

### Typographic Principles
- **Sacred Line Pitch:** Scripture tokens use generous leading to prevent overlapping marks and ensure effortless scanning during oral recitation.
- **Bilingual Harmony:** Font metrics are matched so bilingual numbers, verse markers, and titles maintain horizontal optical alignment.
- **RTL Fluidity:** Arabic text flow maintains standard right-to-left layout rules with proportional glyph expansion rather than aggressive tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a fluid editorial grid anchored around a centered recitation column for deep focus, flanked by auxiliary metrics and schedules.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Mobile (up to 640px):** Single-column canvas with `1.25rem` margin. Bottom floating navigation bar ensures one-handed thumb interaction while holding a prayer bead or reading.
- **Tablet (641px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Splits between the active text block (5 cols) and memory revision status / notes (3 cols).
- **Desktop (1025px+):** 12-column layout max-width constrained to `1280px` centered. Ayah reading pane occupies the golden ratio core (7 columns), flanked by Quranic navigation/surah indexes (2 columns) and daily prayer / revision logs (3 columns).

### Rhythm & Proportions
- Spacing follows an intentional 8-point base rhythm, stepping up to `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) between thematic sections to maintain unhurried, mindful breathing space.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects sharp artificial drop shadows in favor of tonal surface stepping, warm parchment layers, and gold-tinted ambient glows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background substrate (`#FBF9F4` in day mode, `#0B131A` in night mode).
- **Level 1 (Quiet Containers & Cards):** Flat, tonal separation using `#F4EEE1` (or `#111E28`) framed with a 1px border of `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)` (subtle gold leaf contour).
- **Level 2 (Active Recitation & Interactive Cards):** Subtle warm elevation using an ambient shadow tinted with emerald and umber:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(13, 59, 46, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.06);`
- **Level 3 (Modals, Surah Pickers & Overlays):** Frosted ambient backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with a deep tonal scrim, creating an immersive sanctuary overlay.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is soft and architectural, mirroring the rounded archways and smooth stone edges of Islamic architecture while avoiding playful pill forms that detract from reverence.

### Corner Radius System
- **Components & Controls (0.5rem / 8px):** Input fields, standard action buttons, and ayah badges.
- **Cards & Reading Blocks (1rem / 16px):** Surah summary containers, devotional tracking widgets, and memorization cards.
- **Overlays & Floating Sheets (1.5rem / 24px):** Bottom prayer trackers, audio player docks, and modal frames.
- **Micro Accents:** Surah number badges utilize octagonal or gentle 8-point star geometry (*Khatim*) via SVG framing rather than pure circular radiuses.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Wird Confirmation / Start Recitation):** Deep Emerald (`#0D3B2E`) background, warm ivory typography (`#FBF9F4`), padded with `0.75rem 1.75rem`, rounded to `0.5rem`. Subtle gold border hover highlight (`#C5A059`).
- **Secondary Action (Tafsir, Audio Reciter Select):** Subtle parchment surface with a delicate 1px border in `#C5A059` and deep emerald text.
- **Ghost Action:** Zero background, warm gold hover state, minimal ripple.

### Ayah & Wird Cards
- **Recitation Card:** Generous padding (`space-lg`), paper-tinted background, right-to-left orientation, with gold-accented verse markers (ayah end symbols). Selected or repeating verses receive a left-hand (or right-hand in RTL) 3px solid emerald indicator with a faint 5% gold tint fill.
- **Memorization Tracker Node:** Compact card displaying the Juz', Hizb, and Surah name. A circular gold ring gauge illustrates memorization retention percentages (*Mutqin*, *Muraja'a Needed*).

### Chips & Filters
- **Status Chips (e.g., "Saved", "Review Due", "Recited Today"):** Light emerald wash (`rgba(19, 78, 63, 0.08)`) with emerald text and a rounded radius (`0.5rem`). No loud neon indicators; status changes transition through serene jade, warm gold, and terracotta.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Search (Ayah / Topic Finder):** Background matches the base surface with inset borders colored in `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`. Focus state smoothly shifts border color to `#0D3B2E` with a faint warm halo.
- **Recitation Speed & Audio Bar:** Floating bottom-docked audio controller in `#111E28` (dark mode) or `#F4EEE1` (light mode) with gold playhead scrubbers.

### Checkboxes & Devotional Trackers
- **Prayer & Wird Checkboxes:** Custom smooth squares (`rounded: 0.375rem`) bordered in sand gold. On active check, an animated gold checkmark fills with deep emerald interior, providing satisfying micro-affirmation for completed daily tasks.
