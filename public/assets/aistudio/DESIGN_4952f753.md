## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a reverent, quiet luxury rooted in Islamic devotion, classic editorial printing, and contemporary utility. Rather than leaning on excessive pseudo-orientalist filigree or aggressive ornamental patterns, the aesthetic is anchored in pristine whitespace, balanced proportions, artisanal parchment warmth, and deliberate gold accents.

### Visual Pillars
- **Reverence over Spectacle:** The interface recedes to let the sacred text and prayer rhythms command attention. Interactions are soft, deliberate, and free from sensory overload.
- **Modern Manuscript:** Inspired by high-end Islamic lithographs and bespoke Quranic binding, employing subtle hairline rules, restrained metallic gold highlights, and rich vegetal emerald tones.
- **Tactile Serenity:** Surfaces evoke heavyweight cotton paper and warm ivory parchment (`#FAF8F5`) rather than sterile digital white, reducing visual fatigue during prolonged reading cycles.

### Audience & Mood
Engineered for Muslims seeking an undistracted, dignified sanctuary on their digital devices. The UI communicates timeless authority, meditative calm, and flawless modern craft.

## Colors

The palette balances the gravitas of Deep Emerald (`#0F4C3A`) with the ceremonial warmth of Muted Champagne Gold (`#C8A355`), grounded by organic neutrals and deep umber ink.

### Light Mode (Default)
- **Primary (`#0F4C3A`):** Deep Emerald. Applied to core navigational anchors, Surah headers, primary actions, and focal devotional accents.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#C8A355`):** Muted Champagne Gold. Reserved for Ayah markers, active prayer highlights, juz/hizb ornaments, and premium tactile borders.
- **Background (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm Ivory. Prevents retina fatigue, delivering a natural, bookish surface for prolonged recitation.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure Crisp Pearl. Used for cards, elevation layers, and contextual action sheets.
- **Borders (`#E6E0D6`):** Subtle Sandstone. Precise 1px borders that separate cards and sections without harsh contrast.
- **Text Primary (`#1B1E1D`):** Deep Umber Black. Organic ink tone providing maximum legibility without the digital harshness of pure `#000000`.
- **Text Muted (`#65706B`):** Warm Slate. For metadata, Ayah translation details, verse numbers, and secondary timestamps.

### Dark Mode
- **Background:** Deep Charcoal Forest (`#121615`).
- **Surface:** Muted Obsidian (`#1A201E`).
- **Primary:** Luminous Jade (`#1D775C`).
- **Accent:** Subtle Sand Gold (`#D4B26F`).
- **Borders:** Deep Olive Slate (`#27302D`).
- **Text Primary:** Soft Linen (`#E8ECE9`).
- **Text Muted:** Sage Drift (`#8C9993`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes Arabic script legibility and editorial rhythm. Arabic-first rendering requires generous line heights (`lineHeight` set between 1.8x and 2.2x font size for sacred text) to accommodate tashkeel (diacritics), sukun, and madd signs without visual clipping or dense overlap.

### Type Pairings
- **Quranic & Classical Editorial (`Noto Serif` / Amiri / Noto Naskh Arabic):** Dedicated to Surah titles, basmalahs, Ayah bodies, and prominent spiritual titles. Provides crisp calligraphic balance, generous counters, and traditional Naskh metrics.
- **System Interface & Translation (`Plus Jakarta Sans` / Readex Pro):** Handles app navigation, translation text, audio controls, timestamps, settings, and micro-labels with geometric clarity.

### Rules of Composition
- All layout direction is strictly `RTL` (Right-to-Left) by default.
- Punctuation and numbering must follow Eastern Arabic numerals (`١، ٢، ٣`) in Quranic contexts and Western numerals where standard in localized settings.
- Avoid letter spacing on connected Arabic scripts; restrict tracking only to uppercase Latin and secondary Romanized transliteration tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system employs a structured fluid-responsive grid system designed around comfortable hand ergonomics on mobile and refined book spreads on larger viewports.

### Breakpoints & Grids
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column layout with 4-column sub-grid. Outer canvas `margin: 1rem` (16px), column `gutter: 1rem`. Bottom action sheets and docked recitation controls sit within standard finger-reach zones.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Outer canvas `margin: 2rem` (32px), `gutter: 1.5rem`. Enables dual-pane views: Mushaf page on the right, tafsir/translation stream on the left.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout clamped at 1200px width. Outer canvas `margin: 3rem` (48px). Reading spaces emulate the margins of an illuminated folio manuscript.

### Spacing Principles
- Vertical rhythm follows an explicit 4px / 8px scale.
- Verse blocks are insulated by `space-lg` (24px) to preserve cognitive focus and meditative reading cadence.
- Interface controls and metadata chips nest tightly using `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids loud 3D skeuomorphism and heavy drop shadows, relying instead on tonal layering, delicate sandstone hairlines, and ambient parchment diffusion.

### Depth Strategies
- **Primary Plane Separation:** Surfaces rely on color distinction (`#FAF8F5` background vs. `#FFFFFF` surface) framed by a crisp 1px border (`#E6E0D6`).
- **Ambient Shadow (Floating Cards & Sheets):**
  - Blur Radius: `20px`
  - Spread: `0px`
  - Offset: `Y: 4px`
  - Color: `rgba(15, 76, 58, 0.04)` (tinted with Deep Emerald for an organic, luminous falloff instead of muddy grey).
- **Active Devotional Elevation:** When an Ayah or prayer card is active, it receives an ambient gold glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #C8A355, 0 8px 24px rgba(200, 163, 85, 0.12)`).
- **Backdrop Treatments:** Audio players, modal sheets, and floating navigation bars use frosted surface diffusion (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at 92% surface opacity.

## Shapes

The geometric signature combines modern clarity with architectural poise. Standard containers and interactive surfaces employ subtle rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`, where base corners are 0.5rem / 8px, `rounded-lg` is 1rem / 16px, and `rounded-xl` is 1.5rem / 24px).

### Architectural Accents
- **Curvature Hierarchy:** Small interactive elements (buttons, chips, input fields) use 8px–12px radius. Content cards, prayer timeline cards, and Surah listing containers use 16px (`rounded-lg`). Full-screen bottom sheets use 24px (`rounded-xl`) top corner smoothing.
- **Traditional Arabesque Geometries:** Specialized indicators (such as Ayah end markers, Sajdah badges, and Hizb indicators) utilize symmetrical 8-point star medallions or softened octagonal silhouettes rendered as crisp SVG vectors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled with Deep Emerald (`#0F4C3A`), white label typography, 0.5rem roundedness, min-height 48px. In hover/active states, shifts to a deeper forest hue (`#093427`) with soft scale feedback (`transform: scale(0.99)`).
- **Secondary / Gold Accent Button:** Champagne Gold border (`1.5px solid #C8A355`), transparent or subtle tinted background (`rgba(200, 163, 85, 0.06)`), text set in Deep Emerald.
- **Ghost / Icon Button:** Transparent background, Warm Slate icon tone, turning Deep Emerald with a muted circular hover halo (`rgba(15, 76, 58, 0.08)`).

### Chips & Filters
- Compact pill-like badges (0.5rem radius) used for Juz selection, Makki/Madani tags, and recitation speeds.
- Default: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E6E0D6` border, `#65706B` text.
- Selected: `#0F4C3A` background, `#FFFFFF` text, with optional small gold bullet indicator.

### Prayer & Ayah Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E6E0D6` border, and 16px corner radius.
- Includes a dedicated right-aligned metadata header (Surah name, verse reference, Hizb) in Warm Slate typography.
- Active reading state: shifts card border to `#C8A355` with an ultra-subtle gold wash background tint (`rgba(200, 163, 85, 0.03)`).

### Input Fields & Search
- Designed for searching Surahs, root words, and bookmarks.
- 48px height, `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E6E0D6` border, `#1B1E1D` text.
- Focus state: border color transitions to Deep Emerald (`#0F4C3A`) with a subtle 3px focus ring (`rgba(15, 76, 58, 0.12)`). Includes integrated search icon positioned at the right margin for RTL flow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular radios and rounded square checkboxes (4px corner radius).
- Unchecked: `#E6E0D6` border with `#FFFFFF` fill.
- Checked: `#0F4C3A` fill with a clean white checkmark or inner gold accent dot.

### Specialized Devotional Components
- **Floating Reciter Bar:** Docked at the viewport bottom, featuring frosted surface backdrop blur, playback timeline in Champagne Gold, play/pause trigger in Deep Emerald, and audio speed/repeat toggles.
- **Khatmah Progress Tracker:** Minimalist linear gauge utilizing an Ivory track with Champagne Gold progress fill, accompanied by subtle calligraphic milestone indicators.
